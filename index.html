Our story

From grassroots outreach to a pillar of Soweto

Founded in 2020 by Tsholofelo Masike, the Dobsonville Community Foundation began as a small grassroots initiative focused on outreach. As our neighbourhood's challenges grew, so did we.

By 2023 we expanded into skills development and training and moved into our current community centre. Today we serve hundreds of residents every year and remain dedicated to empowering our community from within.
