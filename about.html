About us

Empowering our community from within

We are a Soweto non-profit that grew from grassroots outreach into a community centre serving children, youth, families, seniors and local entrepreneurs.

Our history

How we grew

  1. 2020

    A grassroots beginning

    Tsholofelo Masike founds the Dobsonville Community Foundation as a small outreach initiative in Dobsonville.

  2. 2020 – 2022

    Growing with our neighbourhood

    As local challenges grow, so does our reach: food support, tutoring and mentorship for more families.

  3. 2023

    Skills training and a new home

    We expand into skills development and training and move into our current community centre.

  4. Today

    A proud pillar of Soweto

    We serve hundreds of residents each year and keep building partnerships to expand our programmes.

🎯

Our mission

To uplift Dobsonville residents of all ages through education, wellness programmes and cultural engagement, building a stronger, more self-sustaining community together.

🌅

Our vision

A united, prosperous and empowered Dobsonville for all.

👤

Our founder

Tsholofelo Masike started the foundation in 2020 with a simple belief: Dobsonville has the talent and heart to solve its own challenges when given the right support.

Key objectives

Our goals

Select a goal to see what we do and how it helps.

Educational advancement

We deliver daily after-school tutoring, digital literacy and youth mentorship to local students to improve academic outcomes.

Skills & employment

Each year we train unemployed youth in accredited vocational skills, job-readiness and entrepreneurship so they can find work or create it.

Social & health support

We provide regular food security assistance, health workshops and wellness support to vulnerable families and seniors.

Enterprise growth

We equip local micro-enterprises with business development tools, networking opportunities and mentorship.

Sustainable operations

We secure long-term community partnerships and diversified funding to keep expanding centre programmes and facility capacity.
