Sponsors & donors

Partner with us and grow Dobsonville

Your support turns unemployed youth into skilled workers, keeps learners in school and puts food on the table for vulnerable families, and your brand is recognised for it.

Why partner with us

Real impact you can see and report on

  • Local and trusted: rooted in Dobsonville since 2020 with our own community centre.
  • Measurable results: learners trained, parcels delivered and residents reached, reported back to you.
  • B-BBEE & CSI value: support that can count towards socio-economic development goals.
  • Visible recognition: your logo on our website, centre and event materials.
